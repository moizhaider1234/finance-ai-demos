# 07 - AUDIT lead magnet system (DEMO BUILD, also your own sales tool)
**Problem:** "DM me AUDIT" needs something real behind it.
**How it works:** 7-question form (Never / Sometimes / Always) -> score 0-100 (higher = more manual work to automate) -> tier + personalised, ranked checklist -> PDF (Gotenberg) emailed to the lead -> you get a notification -> lead logged to the `Leads` tab -> two follow-up DRAFTS after 2 and 3 more days.
**Sample:** `sample_checklist.pdf`. Edit the advice text in `scoring.js` to match your real offer.
**Before using on real people:** add a privacy line to the form and a way to opt out, and replace the placeholder emails and signature.
