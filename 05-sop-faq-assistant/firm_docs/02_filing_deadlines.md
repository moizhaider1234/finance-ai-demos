# Firm Deadlines We Work To (demo firm)
- We need all year-end records from you at least 30 days before the filing deadline you have been given.
- Quarterly records are due to us by the 10th day after quarter end.
- Payroll data for a pay run must reach us 3 working days before payday.
- We calculate and show you amounts, but the filing deadlines themselves are set by the tax authority; we confirm your specific dates in your engagement letter.
