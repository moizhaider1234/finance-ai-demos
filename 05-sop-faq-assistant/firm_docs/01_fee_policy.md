# Fee Policy (Meridian Bookkeeping & Tax, demo firm)
- Monthly bookkeeping is charged at a fixed fee agreed in your engagement letter. Fees are invoiced on the 1st of each month and due within 14 days.
- Year-end accounts and tax return preparation are quoted separately and invoiced on completion.
- Work outside the agreed scope is charged at 65 per hour (USD or GBP) and always agreed with you in writing first.
- Late payment: after 30 days overdue we may pause non-statutory work until the balance is cleared.
- Fee changes are notified in writing at least 60 days before they take effect.
