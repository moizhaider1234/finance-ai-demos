# New Client Onboarding (demo firm)
1. Complete the intake form. 2. We send your engagement letter for e-signature. 3. We create your secure folder and send a document checklist. 4. We book a 30-minute kickoff call, usually within 5 working days. 5. We take over bookkeeping from your agreed start date.
Typical onboarding takes 5 to 7 working days once we have your signed letter and first documents.
