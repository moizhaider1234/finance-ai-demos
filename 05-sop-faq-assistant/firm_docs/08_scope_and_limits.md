# What We Do and Do Not Do (demo firm)
We do: bookkeeping, bank reconciliation, management reports, payroll processing, year-end accounts preparation, VAT or sales tax return preparation.
We do not: give personalised tax planning or legal advice in chat or email replies, give investment advice, or act for you with the tax authority without a signed authority form. Tax planning questions are booked as a paid consultation with a qualified accountant.
