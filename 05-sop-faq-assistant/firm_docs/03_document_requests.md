# Documents We Ask Clients For (demo firm)
Monthly: bank statements for every account, credit card statements, sales invoices issued, purchase invoices and receipts, payroll summary.
Year-end: loan statements, fixed asset purchases and disposals, stock count (if applicable), prior year filed accounts.
Send documents through the secure client portal or by email to documents@meridian-demo.example. Please do not send documents by text message.
Scanned or phone-photo receipts are fine if every figure and date is readable.
